# Clinical Trials Analytics Project Contract

## Project scope

Analyse industry-led interventional clinical trials involving DRUG or BIOLOGICAL interventions from 2015 onwards.

The analysis will use a global cohort while applying a UK lens to understand the UK's position within international clinical-trial activity.

## Stakeholder

Clinical Portfolio Strategy / Business Development team within a UK-based pharmaceutical or biotechnology company.

## Decisions supported

The analysis should help the stakeholder:

- Understand how industry-sponsored clinical-trial activity has changed since 2015.
- Identify which countries host the greatest volume of industry trials.
- Assess the UK's position relative to other major trial locations.
- Identify therapeutic areas and intervention types attracting the most industry activity.
- Understand the distribution of trials across clinical phases.
- Identify major industry sponsors and their areas of activity.
- Evaluate the extent to which UK facilities participate in globally sponsored trials.

## Analytical questions

1. How has the number of industry-led interventional DRUG/BIOLOGICAL studies changed since 2015?

2. Which countries host the greatest number of these studies?

3. How does the UK compare with other major countries in terms of trial activity?

4. Which conditions or therapeutic areas have attracted the greatest volume of industry-sponsored research?

5. What is the distribution of studies across Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3 and Phase 4?

6. Which industry sponsors are responsible for the greatest number of studies?

7. Which UK facilities appear most frequently in the selected trial cohort?

8. What proportion of global studies in the cohort include at least one UK location?

## Inclusion criteria

Include studies that:

- Have a study type of INTERVENTIONAL.
- Have at least one DRUG or BIOLOGICAL intervention.
- Are industry-led / have an INDUSTRY lead sponsor.
- Have a study start date from 1 January 2015 onwards.
- Are publicly available through ClinicalTrials.gov / AACT.
- May be conducted in any country.

## Exclusion criteria

Exclude:

- Observational studies.
- Expanded-access records where they do not meet the interventional cohort definition.
- Studies without a DRUG or BIOLOGICAL intervention.
- Studies whose lead sponsor is not classified as INDUSTRY.
- Studies starting before 1 January 2015.
- Records that cannot be reliably assigned to the required study cohort because essential filtering fields are missing.

## Time scope

Start date: 1 January 2015

End date: Data available at the chosen extraction date.

## Geographic scope

Global.

A separate UK lens will be applied using study facilities and country/location data.

## Extract date

25 August 2026

## Important Distinction

Unit of analysis is primarily the study, so if one trial has 5 conditions, 3 interventions and 12 facilities it is still one clinical study.
